Brinkball Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 July 2026

Brinkball has no user accounts, shows no ads, and sells no data. You can play entirely offline.

What is collected

The app includes Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, which collect anonymous usage data (for example: that a game round started or ended, scores, session length) and crash reports, together with a random per-install identifier and basic device information (model, OS version). This data is not linked to your identity and is not used to track you across apps or websites. No advertising identifier (IDFA) is used.

Why

Usage data tells us whether the game works and which parts are fun; crash reports help fix bugs. That's all it is used for.

Game Center

If you choose to sign in to Apple Game Center, leaderboard scores and achievements are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Game Center is optional.

Data retention & your choices

Analytics data is retained by Google Firebase under its standard retention policies. To exercise privacy rights or ask questions, email mail@tommurton.com.

Children

Brinkball is a general-audience game and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
