BRINKBALL

Flirt with the drain. Feast on the score.

How to play

The ember ball is always falling. When it drops into the coloured strip near the bottom, tap anywhere to launch it toward your tap and pop the glass orbs above.

The strip has three bands — ×1, ×2 and the thin red ×4 BRINK just above the drain. The deeper you dare to let the ball fall before flipping, the harder it launches and the more every orb is worth. Three BRINK flips ignite a ten-second ×2 Fever. Three drained balls end the run.
