## Research (multi-agent: genre leaders / juice / difficulty-retention / ASO; 57 findings, sources logged in workflow journal)
- **[research]** Genre leaders (Downwell, Poinpy, Ballz-generation, PinOut, holedown, Super Hexagon, Balatro's HUD economics): first minute must be **unlosable by inaction** (Downwell's safe first drop), teach with ONE wordless prompted action triggered on hesitation (Poinpy's arrow), never gate the economy display (Balatro's always-on chips×mult HUD; Downwell's hidden combo is a documented failure mode), restart must be <2 s (Super Hexagon), and premium buyers paid for zero interruptions between death and retry.
- **[research]** Juice canon (Juice it or lose it, Art of Screenshake, Swink, Peggle postmortems): concentrate ALL top-tier juice on the single player verb (the flip), tier by meaning — squash/stretch scaled by band, hit-stop only on BRINK/fever/final-drain, slow-mo+palette-shift for fever ignition ("Peggle Extreme Fever moment"), confetti for wave clear, count-up never snap, danger = red edge vignette. Reduce Motion: swap shakes for haptic upgrades, keep time changes.
- **[research]** Difficulty/retention: first real loss ~min 3 for median new player; grace mechanics keyed to failure signals only (experts never see them); every drain must be diagnosable; game-over card must answer "how close was I?" (delta-to-best is the near-miss engine); ethical premium retention = mastery visibility + expressive share, never calendar streaks; rating prompt only after a positive moment (new best, 3rd+ session).
- **[research]** ASO 2025-26: first 1-2 screenshots carry nearly all conversion; caption bands (3-7 words, top of frame) are mandatory AND OCR-indexed since June 2025; premium promise ("Pay once / No ads / No IAP / Offline") is both conversion lever and Apple editorial hook — never print the literal price; subtitle is 2nd-strongest ranking field (current subtitle has zero keywords); no new-app launch boost since June 2025 so launch-day page is the page; app preview video ~2× but time-boxed → screenshots first.
